The Starting Reality

Wherever you are starting from, market education usually feels broken

Maybe you are starting from zero. You want to understand how shares and mutual funds work so you can make smarter choices with your savings, yet every online video assumes you already know finance jargon and half the internet sounds like a scam.

Or maybe you have already spent time and real money in the market. You watched hours of tutorials, tried indicators (math formulas drawn on a chart from past prices), or sat through a recorded group course. Still, when a price moves sharply, you cannot explain out loud why it happened.

Neither situation means you cannot learn this. It simply means nobody taught you how a market physically works from the ground up.

SEBI's own studies show how steep the cost of guessing can be. In FY2024-25, roughly 91% of individual traders in equity futures and options lost money, totaling Rs 1,05,603 crore in net losses (SEBI study, July 2025). An earlier SEBI study covering FY2022 to FY2024 found 93% of individual derivatives traders lost over Rs 1.8 lakh crore, and a separate July 2024 study found 7 out of 10 intraday cash traders lost money. Those numbers are not here to scare you away from markets. They show one plain reality: knowing how to click buy on an app is very different from understanding the market underneath it.

The Four-Step Process

How you go from guessing to clear market reasoning in four steps

A stock market is not a secret code. Underneath the numbers, it is a live auction where buyers and sellers constantly negotiate a fair price, much like a busy fish market. Once you see that clearly, every concept builds in a logical order.

1 Week 1

Start where you actually are

In your first live one-on-one session, your mentor maps your exact starting point. Complete beginners start with plain-English basics and everyday analogies. Experienced traders and HNIs skip introductory definitions and begin directly with auction mechanics and market structure.

2 Weeks 2 to 4

Learn how markets work, not just what to click

You study one core idea per session through our nine-part method: why the concept exists, how the exchange matches orders, a real-world analogy, and how to observe a historical chart without jumping to guesses.

3 Practice phase

Practice without risking real money

Before real capital enters the picture, you practice through paper trading (logging practice decisions without risking actual money) and keep a written decision journal. Your mentor reviews your reasoning live, challenges weak assumptions, and helps you build clear risk rules.

4 Ongoing to competence

Build independent judgment

You connect broader economic flows, business analysis, and order flow (the record of actual buying and selling activity behind price movement) into your own repeatable checklist. Mentorship does not stop when a calendar runs out. You continue until you can read a clean chart and explain your reasoning without relying on tips or shortcuts.
