The reality

Market education feels broken at every level.

Maybe you are starting from zero. You want to handle your savings wisely and understand shares or mutual funds, yet every video throws heavy English finance jargon at you and half the internet feels like a scam.

Or maybe you have already spent months and real money on the market. You tried indicators (math formulas drawn on a chart from past prices) or sat through recorded batches, yet when price moves sharply, you still cannot explain out loud why it happened.

Neither means you cannot learn this. It means nobody taught you how a market physically works in your own language before handing you rules.

SEBI’s own studies show how expensive guessing can be. In FY2024–25, roughly 91% of individual equity futures and options traders lost money, totaling Rs 1,05,603 crore in net losses (SEBI study, July 2025). An earlier study covering FY2022 to FY2024 found 93% of individual derivatives traders lost over Rs 1.8 lakh crore, and a July 2024 study found 7 out of 10 intraday equity traders lost money. Those figures are not here to scare you away. They prove one plain truth: tapping buy on an app is very different from understanding the market underneath it.

How you learn

Four clear steps from noise to calm reasoning.

A stock market is not a secret code. Underneath the candles sits a live auction where buyers and sellers constantly negotiate fair value, much like a busy fish market. Learning that mechanism in clear Malayalam removes the fog fast.

01 Step 1 · Already halfway done

Complete your initial assessment.

You meet Chief Mentor Renjith R Pillai live to map your starting level, weekly schedule, and goals. Complete beginners start from zero; experienced traders and HNIs skip basic definitions.

02 Week 1

Understand how markets physically work.

Through everyday analogies (a fish market, a shop counter queue, a shopkeeper’s stock), you grasp how exchanges match orders before touching complex terms.

03 Practice phase

Paper trade with live mentor challenge.

Before risking real savings, you log simulated decisions through paper trading (practice trades using zero real money) and review your written decision journal directly with your mentor.

04 Independent mastery

Read any clean screen calmly.

Picture opening a chart with every indicator switched off, writing a clear risk-capped plan, staying steady during a losing streak, and knowing plainly when to stay out. Those are skills you build step by step until genuine competence is shown.

Anyone can show one lucky screenshot and hide ten poor trades. We do not publish profit claims or promotional testimonials. Instead, real mastery at TRADE SCHOOL is measured by one concrete test and a clear capability shift:

Concrete Mastery Standard

The indicator-free screen test

Before training finishes, your mentor strips every indicator off your practice chart (no RSI, no MACD, no moving averages, no arrows) and asks you to explain what the market is doing, what changed, what proves your read wrong, and how you cap risk. If you cannot answer clearly yet, the mentorship is not finished.
