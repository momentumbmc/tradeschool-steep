Approach & risk disclosure

Education only. Risk always comes first.

Distrust starts wherever financial education blurs into live stock calls or profit hype. Under THC Institute governance, we keep the boundary unmistakable: TRADE SCHOOL teaches market mechanics and calm reasoning in Malayalam, never tips or return promises.

First principles

Why borrowed strategies are not market education.

Indicators and strategies are not conspiracies built to trap retail traders. An indicator is simply a mathematical summary of past data, and a strategy is the final checklist of someone who already understood the market underneath. Trouble starts when an institute sells only the rules without the anatomy behind them, turning rule-following into quiet guesswork the moment market character shifts.

Our goal is intellectual independence: understanding the underlying live auction first, and using tools only when they genuinely clarify your observation or execution.

In class

How safety is practiced every week.

01 · Paper First

Paper trading before live capital

You practice historical chart reads and simulated decisions with zero real savings at risk while disciplined habits settle.

02 · Invalidation First

Falsifiable reasoning

Every market hypothesis must state plainly what observable evidence proves it wrong.

03 · Disciplined Review

Process over outcome

A sloppy trade that gets lucky still gets flagged in review; a well-sized, disciplined decision that takes a planned stop loss gets respected.

04 · SEBI Compliant

Compliant historical market data

Aligned with SEBI’s May 8, 2026 circular (effective July 1, 2026), entities solely engaged in education use market price data with at least a 30-day lag rather than live or recent prices, keeping every lesson strictly structural and free of live security calls.
