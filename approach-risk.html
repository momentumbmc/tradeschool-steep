Our Approach & Risk Disclosure

Education, never tips: how we teach markets and handle risk honestly.

Distrust in stock market education grows wherever lines get blurred. An institute calls itself a school, then quietly pushes live buy alerts, flashes profit screenshots, or implies that one setup removes risk. TradeSchool keeps the line sharp. We teach how financial markets physically work and how to reason under real uncertainty. We never sell stock tips, signals, or return promises.

Intellectual Independence

Understand the market first, then choose your tools

Before you learn another indicator or chart pattern, we ask you to pause on three plain questions: who is moving this market, why might they be doing it, and what observable evidence supports that view?

To stay fair and accurate, indicators and retail strategies are not conspiracies built by institutions to trap retail traders. An indicator is simply a mathematical summary of past data, and a strategy is a set of rules applied to observations. Neither one is the market itself. Problems begin when a learner relies on a rule blindly and freezes the moment market conditions shift. Our goal is intellectual independence, not permanent dependence on a signal or a mentor.

Classroom Risk Architecture

Four classroom standards that put risk first

1 Zero Live Risk Initially

Paper trading before live capital

You practice reading historical charts and logging simulated decisions without risking actual savings while your process takes shape.

2 Scientific Discipline

Falsifiable thinking

Every time you share a market view, your mentor asks what concrete evidence would prove your view wrong. An analysis that cannot be disproved is a wish, not a thesis.

3 Honest Review

Process over lucky outcomes

A sloppy decision that happens to turn out well still gets corrected in review, and a disciplined, well-sized decision that takes a controlled stop loss gets respected.

4 30+ Day Historical Data Lag

Compliant historical market data

In line with SEBI's May 8, 2026 circular (effective July 1, 2026), entities solely engaged in education use market price data with at least a 30-day lag rather than live or recent security prices. Using historical charts keeps every discussion focused strictly on structural skill and ensures education never crosses into live stock calls.

Regulatory Clarity

Plain-language regulatory status and risk disclosure

Universal Expectation Promise

What we promise

A clear, step-by-step curriculum built around your level, plain-English explanations of how markets work, strict risk-management habits, practice before real capital, and honest one-on-one feedback on your reasoning.

Universal Boundary

What we never promise

Profits, fixed returns, specific win rates, buy or sell tips, market signals, or a method that removes uncertainty. Markets carry a real risk of loss, and education does not remove that risk.
