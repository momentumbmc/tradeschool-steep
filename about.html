About TradeSchool

We stay small on purpose, so you actually learn to think for yourself.

Most education companies are built to scale as loudly as possible: huge batches, pre-recorded libraries, fixed calendars, and communities of lakhs of members. TradeSchool was built on the opposite conviction. We teach one student at a time, live, from complete beginner to seasoned HNI, because genuine market judgment grows through dialogue and challenge, never crowded lectures.

01 · Origin & Mission

Why TradeSchool exists

For decades, clear market understanding stayed mostly inside institutional desks and research rooms. Everyday learners in Kerala and across India were handed either dense textbooks disconnected from live auctions or short retail courses focused on memorizing indicators and fixed setups.

We respect anyone learning through books, free videos, or group classes. Still, SEBI's public research between July 2024 and July 2025 showed that 7 out of 10 intraday equity traders and more than 9 out of 10 individual derivatives traders lost money. Knowing how to place an order on a phone app is not the same as understanding the auction underneath it.

TradeSchool exists on a single mission: bringing genuine stock market education to deserving students who want real understanding rather than shortcuts, from a student organizing their first budget to an HNI sharpening institutional analysis.

“One learner who can reason independently in the market is worth more than a hundred who are permanently searching for the next signal.”

TradeSchool · Core Operating Conviction
An Ethical Operating Limit

Why quality over quantity is a hard constraint here

Running TradeSchool one-on-one makes the business slower to grow and harder to scale. We accept that limit on purpose as an ethical position, not a slogan.

What mass-volume institutes optimize for What TradeSchool optimizes for
Building giant communities of lakhs of learners Training a small circle who genuinely practice real market learning
Filling large batches every month Individual diagnosis for one student at a time
Finishing a fixed syllabus on a calendar date Continuing until genuine competence is demonstrated
Handing over a teacher's fixed strategy Building independent reasoning and research habits
Keeping learners subscribed to alerts Making sure you need your mentor less over time
