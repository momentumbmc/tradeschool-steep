Mission

Replacing borrowed strategies with real market literacy.

For decades, real auction understanding stayed inside institutional desks while everyday learners in Kerala received indicators, candlestick memorization, and crowded classes. Between July 2024 and July 2025, SEBI’s public research recorded that 7 out of 10 intraday equity traders and over 9 out of 10 individual derivatives traders lost money. Knowing how to tap buy on a phone is nowhere near understanding the market underneath.

Guided by Chief Mentor Renjith R Pillai’s 16-year methodology and staffed by a senior faculty with 10+ years of experience each, TRADE SCHOOL exists on one clear mission: bringing genuine, mechanics-first market education in clear Malayalam to deserving students in Kerala and Malayalees across the globe, whether they are organizing household finances or refining institutional-grade research.
