No Fixed Static Fee Table

Why TradeSchool uses dynamic pricing instead of a fixed fee table

TradeSchool does not publish a static fee structure. Because every mentorship session is taught live and one-on-one directly by our lead mentor without delegating to junior staff, available teaching capacity shifts continuously.

Our program fees operate on a dynamic price range based on three honest factors:

Factor 1

Current mentor availability and demand

When active one-on-one student slots are nearly full, fees reflect tighter mentor bandwidth; when new capacity opens, the range adjusts accordingly.

Factor 2

Your selected program track

Fees differ between our beginner Personal Finance and Market Foundations track, our comprehensive 11-level flagship mentorship (The Art of Making Wealth), and custom corporate or campus workshops.

Factor 3

Your starting scope

Because we map your syllabus around where you stand today, your mentor confirms the exact scope you need before quoting a number.

Balanced Overview

A fair comparison with other ways to learn financial markets

One-on-one mentorship is not the only legitimate way to study markets. Here is a balanced comparison so you can decide what matches your stage and budget best.

Learning path Typical cost in India What it does well Where people usually get stuck
Free YouTube videos and broker webinars Rs 0 Helpful for learning basic vocabulary and exploring initial interest Contradictory opinions, no clear sequence, and nobody checking your reasoning before you act
Books and self-study Rs 1,500 to Rs 6,000 for a solid reading shelf Deep, lasting principles at very low cost Hard to bridge textbook concepts onto real market behavior alone with zero feedback on blind spots
Formal certifications (NISM exams, university diplomas) Rs 1,500 per NISM exam to Rs 1 lakh+ for degree tracks Strong regulatory, compliance, and academic credentials for finance careers Built around exam syllabi rather than live auction reading, order flow, or personal execution habits
Standard batch trading institutes Often Rs 15,000 to Rs 75,000+ across separate intraday, swing, and options courses Peer classroom energy and short fixed timelines One rigid speed for everyone (too fast for beginners, too shallow for HNIs), heavy reliance on indicators, and support ends when the calendar stops
Trial and error with real money Unpredictable (SEBI's July 2025 study recorded Rs 1.056 lakh crore in aggregate net retail F&O losses in FY24-25) Immediate emotional feedback The costliest teacher available, often cementing anxious habits before real understanding forms
TradeSchool (1-on-1 live mentorship) Dynamic range confirmed in writing on your free intro call Tailored to your exact level (beginner to HNI), direct mentorship from a 16-year practitioner, paper trading before live risk, and 11 levels spanning mechanics, mutual funds, and business analysis Demands active study, honest weekly effort, and patience; never offers quick tips or fixed return promises
