Availability-based model

Priced by real faculty capacity, never gimmicks.

Because every student at TRADE SCHOOL is taught live and one on one by senior practitioners with 10+ years of experience rather than junior staff, available teaching bandwidth shifts continuously. Fees therefore follow a dynamic price range shaped by three clear factors:

Factor 01

Your starting scope

Beginner Foundations, flagship 11-level mentorship, and institutional workshops carry distinct depth and hours.

Factor 02

Current demand and senior faculty availability

Tighter active student capacity adjusts the current range openly.

Factor 03

Exact written confirmation upfront

Before you spend a rupee, your senior advisor shares your exact current fee and written terms with zero hidden software markups, forced alert subscriptions, or surprise renewals afterward.

The Assessment Gate

Complete clarity before continuing into full training.

Rule 01

Admission only after initial assessment

After your free 30-minute intro call, a dedicated 10+ year assessment specialist conducts your initial assessment sessions so we verify fit and starting level accurately.

Rule 02 · Protected Window

100% refund right after assessment sessions

Once you complete the initial assessment sessions, you may quit the training and receive a 100% refund.

Rule 03

Clear boundary thereafter

Once you decide to continue past the assessment sessions into regular training, no refunds apply. Every condition is confirmed plainly in writing before payment.
