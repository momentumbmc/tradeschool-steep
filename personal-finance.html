Sample lesson

Picture a vegetable market.

Farmers bring produce, households come to buy, and both sides negotiate until they agree on a price. A financial market runs the exact same way, trading small ownership slices of companies (shares), loans (bonds), and currencies.

If a company has one crore shares and you own one, you own one ten-millionth of that business. Nobody sets the price by decree. Every second on NSE or BSE, buyers and sellers negotiate fair value. Millions of those agreements create a live continuous auction.

Investing means holding business ownership for years. Trading means acting on shorter price moves over days or hours. Mixing up those two games is one of the fastest ways beginners lose savings.
