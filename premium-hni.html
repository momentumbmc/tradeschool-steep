The unspoken problem

Blind trust is the most expensive habit in wealth.

You check every major contract, vendor margin, and critical decision inside your business or profession. Yet when it comes to your own hard-earned surplus, almost every accomplished person falls into the same trap: handing capital to a bank relationship manager, a fund distributor, or occasional guesswork on a mobile app, and hoping it stays safe.

Before assuming your money is properly protected, test your own position against four quiet questions:

  1. 01

    Do you know whether your mutual funds sit in Regular or Direct plans, and how much embedded trail commission quietly leaves your portfolio every single year?

  2. 02

    If you hold six or eight schemes, do you know how many own the exact same underlying stocks while charging you multiple fees?

  3. 03

    When was the last time anyone reviewed your downside risk and removed lagging funds without earning a commission to move your money?

  4. 04

    When interest rates climb, inflation rises, or global currencies shift, do you know how your business surplus, equities, debt, gold, and cross-border savings are exposed?

Private Self-Check: 0 of 4 verified

Bank desks and distributors earn from the products they place, never from your independence. You do not need to become an active trader to protect serious capital, and any tax-sensitive shift from Regular to Direct holdings must always be planned alongside your Chartered Accountant. Yet without your own independent yardstick, you cannot tell whether advice serves your family or someone else’s sales target.

And when sharp professionals step into equities or active markets directly without understanding how prices actually move, trial and error turns expensive fast. SEBI’s own public studies show 91% of individual equity derivatives traders incurred net losses of Rs 1.056 lakh crore in FY2024-25, 93% lost over Rs 1.8 lakh crore across FY2022 to FY2024, and 7 in 10 intraday equity cash traders lost money.

True financial control begins when you can verify everything.
