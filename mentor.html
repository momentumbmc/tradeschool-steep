Curriculum architect

From stock market research analyst to quiet mentor.

Renjith R Pillai brings 16 years of hands-on experience in financial markets. He began his career as a stock market research analyst, examining businesses, market data, and institutional behavior from the inside, before becoming a professional trader who developed his own structured trading methods across changing market cycles.

Across Kerala and among Malayalees working worldwide, he watched capable people lose savings and confidence after paying for crowded batches that taught borrowed setups and indicators as “market education.” When market character changed, those rules broke and nobody could explain why.

That lived reality shaped TRADE SCHOOL’s core mission: establishing genuine, mechanics-first stock market education in Kerala for deserving students. He has zero interest in building a community of lakhs. His focus is guiding a small, disciplined circle of learners who truly practice independent market reasoning.

In market analysis, his methodology starts on the higher timeframe, maps where institutions are likely to draw liquidity, and brings that read down to the lower timeframe for disciplined execution, relying on recognizing what large participants are doing rather than guessing headlines.
